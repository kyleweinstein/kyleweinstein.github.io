Present state and mechanism

Fixed in this lab: company capacity 200 kt, 50m shares, 6% interest, 25% tax on profits only (losses get no tax credit), variable costs +4% and fixed costs +3% to the forecast date, utilisation capped at 95%. k is the % change in price for each 1-point change in industry utilisation.

Move probability between A and C

Each state is valued as a whole enterprise (EBIT × multiple, or the distress value if higher). Net debt is rolled forward by the net income earned on the way, lenders are paid first, and shareholders get what is left, which can be zero. The break-even probability holds your own values and your B-to-C odds fixed, so it tests your model and is not a pure reading of the market; it shows as not defined when A is not the favourable state. One-period, undiscounted snapshot; multiples and distress value are judgements.

Score

Brier score = (probability − outcome)², averaged; lower is better and always saying 50% scores 0.250, which is the benchmark to beat. The observed-rate comparison uses a frequency only known afterward, so it is a hindsight check and is blank when every outcome is the same. With fewer than about ten predictions per bin, read the calibration table as a habit-builder, not a verdict.
